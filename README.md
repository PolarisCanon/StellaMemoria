# StellaMemoria
Stella Memoria (Memory of the Stars)　
Religious choral piece “Stella Memoria (Memory of the Stars)” Style: A fusion of the sound of Mozart’s “Requiem” (melancholic D minor) with the serene beauty of “Lilium” Tonality: D minor (d-moll / a key expressing deep sorrow and solemnity) Tempo: Andante lacrimoso (tearfully, slowly) [Lyrics (Latin and English translation)] (*The pipe organ and low-pitched string instruments play a quiet, heavy intro, expanding from a soprano solo into a grand mixed chorus) Kyrie, eleison. Stellae aeternae, (Lord, have mercy. Eternal stars,) In silentio spatii, flemus et oramus. (In the silence of space, we weep and pray.) Vita brevis est, ut flos lilii, (Life is short, fleeting like a white lily,) Sed gloria animae in caelum volat. (But the glory of the soul flies up to heaven.) Requiem aeternam dona eis, Domine, (Grant them eternal rest, O Lord,) Et lux perpetua luceat eis. (And let perpetual light shine upon them.) Gemini3 created this for me in response to my request.
